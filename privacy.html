Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Jozour Poster is a personal automation tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok and Instagram accounts.

What the tool accesses

When the owner connects TikTok, the tool receives an access token that lets it identify the connected account and upload videos to it. No other person is asked to log in, and no data about other users is collected.

How data is stored

The access token and the videos are stored only on the owner's own computer. Videos are sent only to TikTok (and Instagram) in order to be posted. The tool does not sell, share, or send any data to anyone else, and it has no analytics or advertising.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok's settings (Security and permissions, Manage app permissions) or by deleting the stored token.
